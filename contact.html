Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common travel queries.

How do I book a tour package?

Simply fill in the contact form above or call us. Our travel expert will get in touch within 24 hours to discuss your requirements and share a customised itinerary and quote.

What is included in the package price?

Each package clearly lists what's included (accommodation, meals, transfers, flights, etc.). We ensure complete transparency — no hidden charges whatsoever.

Can I customise a package?

Absolutely! All our packages can be fully customised to match your budget, travel dates, group size and personal preferences.

What is the cancellation policy?

We offer flexible cancellation options. Cancellations made 30+ days before departure receive a full refund. Cancellations within 7–30 days attract a partial charge. Please contact us for specific terms.

Do you provide visa assistance?

Yes! We provide complete visa assistance for international packages including document preparation, application tracking and guidance throughout the process.
