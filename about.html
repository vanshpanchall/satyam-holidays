Our Story

Turning Travel Dreams Into Lasting Memories

Founded in 2010, Satyam Holidays was born from a simple belief: every person deserves a holiday that feels magical. What started as a small boutique travel agency in New Delhi has grown into one of India's most trusted tourism brands, serving over 50,000 happy travellers across 120+ destinations worldwide.

Our team of passionate travel experts brings first-hand knowledge, personalised service and meticulous planning to every journey — whether it's a serene honeymoon in the Maldives, a thrilling Himalayan trek or a grand European tour.

  • ISO-certified travel management company
  • Award-winning customer service — 3 consecutive years
  • IATA accredited travel agency
  • Partnered with 500+ hotels & resorts worldwide
  • 98% customer satisfaction rate
